# KUBERA — AI Work Partner

## What KUBERA is

**KUBERA is a digital work partner that helps take a real task from a plain-language request to a useful, checked result — while the human stays in control.**

It is not meant to be another chatbot that only produces an answer. KUBERA is designed around completing useful work: researching, checking information, comparing options, preparing documents and responses, organising findings, and proposing the next action.

## The problem

People already have search engines, AI chats and dozens of specialist tools. The difficult part is still turning all of that information into a reliable result.

A small-business owner may need to understand why customers are disappearing. A self-employed professional may need to review many opportunities and prepare replies. A public-service worker may need to find the correct official service for a resident. A researcher may need to compare conflicting sources before making a decision.

Today this often means switching between websites, AI tools, documents and manual checks. KUBERA is intended to bring that work into one controlled process.

## The simple KUBERA workflow

**You describe the task → KUBERA researches and checks → KUBERA prepares the result → you decide what happens next.**

The client does not need to understand the internal AI system. The experience should feel like working with one capable digital partner.

## What KUBERA can help with

### Small business

**Example request:** “Why are we getting fewer customers? Check our website, reviews and competitors and tell me what we should improve.”

KUBERA can collect relevant information, compare competitors, identify recurring customer feedback and prepare an evidence-based action plan.

**Client receives:** findings, supporting sources, priorities and proposed next steps.

### Self-employed professionals

**Example request:** “Review these opportunities, identify the ones that fit me and prepare replies.”

KUBERA can organise opportunities, compare them with the user's requirements and prepare personalised drafts for review.

**Client receives:** a shortlist and ready-to-review responses instead of manually processing every item.

### Public and community services

**Example request:** “A resident needs help with housing. Find the relevant service and official information.”

KUBERA can help identify relevant services, locate official sources and prepare a clear next step for the person or staff member assisting them.

**Client receives:** a clearer route to the appropriate service with source information rather than an invented answer.

KUBERA is an assistance and information tool. It should not impersonate a public authority or make official eligibility decisions on its behalf.

### Research and intelligence

**Example request:** “Research this technology, compare the important developments and tell me what matters for our project.”

KUBERA can investigate multiple sources, distinguish evidence from commentary, identify contradictions and produce a structured briefing.

**Client receives:** a usable report rather than a pile of search results.

### Documents and business communication

**Example request:** “Use our existing information and prepare a proposal for this customer.”

KUBERA can collect the relevant project context and prepare a structured draft for human review.

**Client receives:** a prepared document instead of starting from a blank page.

### Market and competitor monitoring

**Example request:** “What are customers saying about us and our competitors, and where are we losing?”

KUBERA can organise public feedback and competitor information, identify recurring themes and prepare practical observations.

**Client receives:** a structured comparison and possible actions to investigate.

## What makes KUBERA different

The goal is not to compete with individual AI models. KUBERA can use AI as part of a larger work process while keeping the experience centred on the user's task.

The product is built around five client-facing principles:

1. **Work, not just answers** — the goal is a usable result.
2. **Evidence where it matters** — important findings should be traceable to their sources.
3. **Human control** — important external actions remain subject to the user's decision.
4. **Project continuity** — work can continue from existing project context instead of restarting every time.
5. **One working relationship** — research, checking, preparation and follow-up should feel like one service rather than many disconnected tools.

## What the client should see

The interface should stay simple.

A task can show:

- what KUBERA is working on;
- what information has been found;
- which findings are supported and which remain uncertain;
- the prepared result;
- proposed next actions;
- an approval step when an external action matters.

The complexity belongs behind the product, not in front of the customer.

## Human control

KUBERA should distinguish between **preparing an action** and **performing an action**.

For example, it can prepare an email, public post, document change or business response. The user can review it before anything important is sent or published.

This principle is central to the product:

> **KUBERA prepares and assists. The human remains the authority.**

## Initial customer groups

The product direction is relevant to:

- small businesses;
- self-employed professionals;
- researchers and analysts;
- community organisations and NGOs;
- teams dealing with information-heavy workflows;
- public-service discovery and staff-assisted information use cases.

The first commercial version should focus on a small number of repeatable jobs rather than trying to automate every type of work.

## Product direction

The long-term idea is one KUBERA work partner that can operate across different interfaces — web, chat, voice and business communication channels — while retaining the same project context and human-control principles.

Different AI technologies can change underneath it. The customer relationship should remain consistent.

## Positioning

> **KUBERA turns a real-world task into checked, organised and actionable work — with the human in control.**

Or even more simply:

> **You give KUBERA the task. KUBERA does the groundwork. You make the decision.**

## Current status

KUBERA is under active development and experimentation. The concepts and examples on this page describe the product direction; they should not be read as claims that every workflow or integration is already production-ready.
