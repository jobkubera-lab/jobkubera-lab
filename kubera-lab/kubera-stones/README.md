# KUBERA STONES

**Craft concept — separate from the KUBERA / DZAMBALA runtime.**

KUBERA STONES are one-of-a-kind handmade decorative stones created to a client idea.

Possible personal elements include:

- birthday and zodiac symbolism;
- a birth-year coin or another agreed coin element;
- a time symbol;
- a zipper / closure motif;
- custom composition requested by the client.

## Sizes

- **Small desk** — compact personal or desk piece.
- **Medium interior** — decorative interior piece.
- **Large hall / exhibition** — larger statement piece for halls, displays or exhibitions.

Each piece is custom-made rather than mass-produced. A client can propose the central idea, symbols and visual direction for the order.

When a crowdfunding campaign or campaign reward is discussed, delivery is **UK only** unless the campaign terms explicitly say otherwise.

KUBERA STONES are artistic and decorative objects. They do **not** promise income, financial gain, luck, prosperity, medical effects or supernatural results.
