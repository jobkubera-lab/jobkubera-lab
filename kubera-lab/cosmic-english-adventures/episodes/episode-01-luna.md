# Episode 01 — Meet Luna

## Goal
Teach a child the first four English concepts through one character: **HELLO, MOON, WHITE, NIGHT**.

## Duration
2–3 minutes.

## Scene 1 — Hello
Luna appears in a starry sky.

**Luna:** “Hello! I’m Luna.”

On screen:
- HELLO
- LUNA

Child repeats: **Hello, Luna!**

## Scene 2 — Moon
Luna points to herself.

**Luna:** “I am the Moon. Moon.”

On screen:
- MOON

Repeat three times: slow → normal → child pause.

## Scene 3 — White
Luna glows white.

**Luna:** “I am white. White.”

On screen:
- WHITE

Colouring connection: the matching workbook page contains the word **WHITE** inside Luna’s moon body.

## Scene 4 — Night
Stars appear.

**Luna:** “It is night. Night.”

On screen:
- NIGHT

## Mini game
Luna asks:
- “Where is the Moon?”
- “What colour is Luna?”

Visual answers appear after a short pause.

## Final repeat
HELLO → MOON → WHITE → NIGHT

**Luna:** “Well done! See you next time!”

## Learning rule
No slang, glottal-stop theory or advanced pronunciation terminology in this children’s track. The child first learns clear, useful English. Natural British pronunciation can be introduced gradually in a separate level.
