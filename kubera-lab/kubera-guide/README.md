## What the project represents

Kubera Guide is more than a collection of photos. It is a growing field record of real places across countries and cities, created through direct observation rather than desk research.

The workflow is simple:

1. Visit a place in person.
2. Photograph and observe the location.
3. Share useful visual and practical information on Google Maps.
4. Learn what people actually view and find useful.
5. Apply that field knowledge to independent civic-tech and mapping projects.

## Connection to KUBERA LAB

The experience behind Kubera Guide supports projects such as the **Mitcham Survival Map** and future community mapping tools. Real-world observation helps make digital maps more practical, human and useful.
