## 👋 About me

I build practical tools from complex, messy information: I find the official sources, connect them, check them, and put the result in front of the people who need it — with the source next to every fact.

My focus is **UK property and location data**. I work with HM Land Registry, energy certificates, Ordnance Survey addresses (UPRN), Environment Agency flood maps, planning and council data, and I build **AI agents** that use these sources without inventing answers.

- 🏠 Building **KUBERA GEO SI** — a live tool for estate agencies in Mitcham, London
- 🤖 Designing AI assistants that show their sources and leave decisions to a person
- 🗺️ Mapping: Google Maps contributor and OpenStreetMap volunteer
- 🌱 Contributing to UK public-sector open source (GOV.UK, NHS, LocalGov Drupal)
- 💼 Looking for **remote or flexible work** in data, GIS, PropTech, AI tools or project coordination

## 🚀 Featured project — KUBERA GEO SI

<table>
<tr>
<td width="55%" valign="top">

**Property intelligence for UK estate agencies**

For every home it prepares the facts an agent has to give a buyer — from open government data, with sources.

- 📍 Exact address point and energy certificate (UPRN)
- 📋 Material Information pack, marked *from data* / *ask the seller*
- 🌊 Flood, conservation area, listed buildings, crime, noise
- 💷 Stamp Duty, mortgage, running costs, agency fee
- ⚖️ Legal documents check: what is confirmed, missing or conflicting
- 💬 Built-in AI assistant answering from official data
