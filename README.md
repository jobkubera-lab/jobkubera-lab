## About me

I design and build AI agents and data tools that work on real, checkable information. I start from an idea, turn it into a working product, and test it until it is reliable.

My main field is **property and location data in the UK**: I connect open government sources — HM Land Registry, energy certificates, flood maps, planning and council data — and make them useful for people who work with homes every day.

I care about two things in every project: **the source of every fact is visible**, and **a person stays in control** of important decisions.

## What I am looking for

**Remote work** in data, GIS, AI tools or AI agents — employment or project-based.
Open to UK teams, councils and public-sector suppliers, prop-tech and AI start-ups.

## What I do

- **AI agents** — assistants that use tools, follow rules and show their sources
- **Data products** — collecting, cleaning and matching UK open data (addresses, UPRN, EPC, sales, risks)
- **Maps** — interactive maps and map layers for property and local areas
- **Document checks** — reading PDFs and finding what is confirmed, missing or conflicting
- **Testing and automation** — automated checks and scheduled data updates
